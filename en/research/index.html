RESEARCH

Research program

Exciton–polaritons offer a versatile platform for engineering strong, tunable interactions in integrated photonic systems. This program connects fundamental studies of polariton physics with the development of manufacturable devices and circuit architectures for nonlinear and quantum photonics.

01

RESEARCH AREA

Quantum materials

Explore excitonic states, polaronic effects, and correlated phases in 2D materials, van der Waals heterostructures, and moiré superlattices. Use steady-state and ultrafast spectroscopy to uncover their formation, dynamics, and tunability.

  • Moiré excitons and polarons
  • Correlated quantum states
  • Cavity-coupled superlattices
  • Ultrafast optical response
02

RESEARCH AREA

Exciton–polaritons

Study exciton–polariton formation, propagation, condensation, and many-body interactions in 2D semiconductors, moiré materials, and other quantum materials. Engineer microcavities, materials, and external fields to enhance nonlinearities and advance all-optical switching, quantum light sources, and on-chip polaritonic devices.

  • Few-quanta nonlinearities
  • Spin-dependent interactions
  • Electrical tuning and injection
  • Polariton circuits
03

RESEARCH AREA

Micro- and Nanophotonics

Design photonic crystals and nanophotonic structures hosting bound states in the continuum (BICs), topological states, and flat bands to control optical dispersion, the local density of optical states, and light–matter coupling. Connect polaritonic units through coupled cavity arrays for coordinated on-chip operation.

  • Photonic-crystal BICs
  • Topological and flat bands
  • Coupled-cavity arrays
  • Robust on-chip routing
04

RESEARCH AREA

AI for polaritonics

Apply physics-informed machine learning, inverse design and closed-loop experiments to optimize polaritonic lattices, from material and cavity selection to integrated devices. Explore polaritonic systems as a platform for energy-efficient computing.

  • Physics-informed learning
  • Photonic inverse design
  • Closed-loop experiments
  • Lattice-scale optimization

METHODS

From materials to integrated devices

01

Quantum materials & emergent states

Assemble low-dimensional quantum materials and van der Waals heterostructures to probe excitons and correlated quantum phases.

02

Nanofabrication & device integration

Develop micro- and nanofabrication methods and integration strategies for optical microcavities, photonic crystals and on-chip polaritonic devices.

03

Multidimensional spectroscopy

Map energy, momentum, spin and dynamics through angle-, polarization-, time- and spatially resolved spectroscopy.

04

Many-body theory & modeling

Model strong coupling, nonlinearities, many-body interactions and nonequilibrium dynamics through theory and numerical simulation.

05

AI for joint optimization

Apply physics-informed learning, inverse design and iterative experiments to refine materials, structures and experimental parameters together.
