PRINCIPAL INVESTIGATOR

Jiaxin Zhao

Researcher under the Hundred Talents Program (Assistant Professor) · Department of Physics · Zhejiang University

Jiaxin Zhao investigates light–matter interactions in low-dimensional semiconductors, with a focus on excitons, exciton–polaritons and microcavity photonics. Her work spans nonlinear optical processes, room-temperature polariton condensation and lasing, and ultrafast dynamics, connecting fundamental exciton physics with integrated photonic and quantum technologies.

She earned her Ph.D. in Physics from Nanyang Technological University (NTU) and received the NTU Presidential Postdoctoral Fellowship and the Chinese Government Award for Outstanding Self-Financed Students Abroad. She has published 27 papers with more than 3,500 citations, including 11 first- or co-first-author papers in Nature Photonics, Nature Nanotechnology, Nature Communications, Light: Science & Applications and Nano Letters. She leads an independent project supported by the NTU Presidential Postdoctoral Fellowship and has served as a reviewer for Nature Physics, Nature Communications, ACS Nano, Nano Letters and Advanced Optical Materials.
