RESEARCH PROGRAM

Four complementary research areas

We link advances in quantum materials and microcavity polaritons to integrated platforms for nonlinear and quantum photonics.

01

Quantum materials

Explore excitonic states, polaronic effects, and correlated phases in 2D materials, van der Waals heterostructures, and moiré superlattices.

Moiré excitonsCorrelated states2D heterostructures
↗02

Exciton–polaritons

Investigate polariton formation, propagation, condensation, and many-body interactions in 2D semiconductors, moiré materials, and other quantum materials.

Strong couplingQuantum nonlinearitiesElectrical control
↗03

Micro- and Nanophotonics

Design photonic crystals and nanostructures with BICs, topological states, and flat bands to control light–matter interactions and build on-chip polaritonic systems.

Photonic crystalsBICsTopological lattices
↗04

AI for polaritonics

Apply physics-informed machine learning, inverse design and closed-loop experiments to optimize polaritonic lattices and integrated devices.

Inverse designAutomated experimentsScalable optimization
